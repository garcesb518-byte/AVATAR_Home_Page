Why AC systems talk about power factor.

Most campus equipment operates on alternating current. Some devices exchange energy with electric or magnetic fields, so current can be larger than the portion doing useful average work.

Explore real, reactive, and apparent power Open interactive explanation +

Real power (P) is the average power converted into work, heat, light, or motion. Reactive power (Q) supports electric and magnetic fields. Apparent power (S) represents the combined electrical loading.

Real power: P, measured in kW
Reactive power: Q, measured in kvar
Apparent power: S, measured in kVA
Power factor: PF = P ÷ S
