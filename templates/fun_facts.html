Fun Facts

Energy by the numbers

Stats about AI, showers, solar, and dorm life that put Villanova's energy footprint in perspective.

Biggest opportunity

The shower problem

Energy per 10-min shower 2–5 kWh

Equivalent to leaving a light bulb on for an entire day.

Villanova spends on showers ~$900,000/yr

Across all residence halls combined, hot water is one of the largest line items.

Savings from 2 fewer minutes ~$200,000/yr

If every on-campus student cut their daily shower by just 2 minutes.

The single highest-impact behavioral change on campus

No infrastructure required — just awareness. That's what AVATAR is built to create.

AI & technology

The hidden cost of digital life

AI query vs. Google search 10× more energy

One ChatGPT prompt uses roughly 10 times the energy of a standard web search.

Global AI data centers by 2026 ~1,000 TWh/yr

Projected annual consumption — comparable to Japan's entire national grid.

Streaming video for 1 hour ~0.08 kWh

Equal to leaving an LED bulb on for 3 hours. Small per stream, enormous at scale.

Dorm life

What's draining your room

Mini-fridge annual draw ~350 kWh/yr

One of the biggest single energy draws in any dorm room — running 24/7 all semester.

Idle charger "vampire load" Up to 10%

Phone and laptop chargers left plugged in but unused can account for up to 10% of your room's total usage.

PC left on overnight all semester = 3 months of hallway LEDs

Leaving a desktop on overnight every night uses as much energy as lighting an entire hallway for 3 months.

Charging your phone daily for a year Only ~2 kWh total

Less than one load of laundry. Your phone isn't the problem — it's everything else.

Small habits, large collective impact

Villanova has over 3,000 on-campus residents. A 2-minute shower reduction, unplugged chargers, and powered-down PCs — multiplied by 3,000 people — adds up to hundreds of thousands of dollars in savings annually.

Solar potential

What PV panels can do

One solar panel generates 300–400 kWh/yr

Enough to power a full-size refrigerator for an entire year.

CO₂ saved per panel lifetime ~1 ton

Each panel prevents approximately one metric ton of CO₂ emissions over its 25-year lifespan.

AVATAR's projected PV savings ~$62k/yr

Our modeled rooftop PV proposal accounts for 86% of total projected annual savings once implemented.
