About AVATAR

Advancing Villanova to Energy Autonomy and Resiliency

AVATAR is a campus energy visualization and decision-support project. Its goal is to make energy information easier to understand and help the Villanova community explore practical paths toward sustainability and resilience.

Built to grow

The homepage provides the shared foundation for future analytical tools, approved data connections, expanded building views, and research-driven forecasting models.
