Built for the Villanova community

Villanova University is the nation’s only Augustinian Catholic university. Its personalized academic experience brings faculty and students together as partners in learning, research, and scholarly inquiry.

Located just outside Philadelphia, Villanova provides opportunities for hands-on research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and experiential learning. AVATAR carries that spirit into the university’s energy future by making campus information easier to understand and explore.
