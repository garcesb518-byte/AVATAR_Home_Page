Data & methodology

Useful energy insight, without publishing private readings.

AVATAR combines approved internal information with public weather and solar resources, then presents only normalized, aggregated, or model-generated campus estimates.

✓

Facilities measurements remain confidential

Raw building-level interval data are used only as approved internal model inputs. They are not displayed, downloadable, or returned to website visitors. Public pages show campus-scale planning estimates and derived relationships rather than the underlying Facilities records.

Processing approach

From protected inputs to public estimates

01

Prepare

Clean approved measurements, remove incomplete periods, and convert the usable history into normalized relationships.

02

Model

Combine normalized energy behavior with public weather and calendar features to create the next-day campus estimate.

03

Communicate

Publish only campus-scale forecasts, scenario results, status information, and explanations suitable for public use.
