Why AVATAR?

Make campus energy easier to see, understand, and improve

Make campus energy information easier to find, understand, and discuss through the website and its data tools. Use energy data and forecasts to help the Villanova community explore more efficient choices and their possible effects. Help the university understand and plan for energy demand, with the long-term goal of supporting reliable campus operations.

The future of energy

A changing energy future.
A shared responsibility.

The future of energy calls for reliable power with a lower environmental impact. Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases, making energy efficiency and cleaner sources of power important parts of that future. Understanding how and when buildings use energy helps communities explore those choices. At Villanova, that begins with making campus energy information accessible and useful.

Our mission

Understanding today.
Empowering tomorrow.

AVATAR stands for Advancing Villanova to Energy Autonomy and Resiliency. Our mission is to make campus energy easier to understand and use that knowledge to support a more sustainable, resilient Villanova. Through accessible data, load forecasting, and scenario exploration, our student-led project aims to connect students, faculty, and campus partners with information that supports informed energy decisions and long-term planning.
