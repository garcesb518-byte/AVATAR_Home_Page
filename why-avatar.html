Why AVATAR?

Make campus energy easier to see, understand, and improve

AVATAR stands for Advancing Villanova to Energy Autonomy and Resiliency. It is a student-led effort focused on energy transparency, sustainability, resiliency, and better access to information.

◉

Transparency

Turn complex campus energy data into information people can understand.

↗

Planning

Use forecasting and scenario analysis to explore where campus energy may be headed.

◆

Resiliency

Support informed conversations about a stronger and more adaptable energy system.
