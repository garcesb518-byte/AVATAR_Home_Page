About AVATAR

Advancing Villanova to Energy Autonomy and Resiliency

AVATAR is a student-led campus energy visualization and decision-support project focused on making energy information more understandable, accessible, and useful.

Our purpose

AVATAR gives the Villanova community greater visibility into campus energy use so students, faculty, facilities staff, and decision-makers can understand present conditions and explore possibilities for the future.

Built to grow

The project provides a shared foundation for approved data connections, building-level views, research-driven forecasting models, and sustainability scenario tools.
