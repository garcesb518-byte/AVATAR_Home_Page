Measured data

Bartley Hall at a glance

Bartley Hall's meter recorded an average load of about 507 kW, or roughly 12,155 kWh per day, from December 2025 to October 2026. Its peak demand was 884 kW on September 1, 2026 at 4:30 PM. Between 1 and 4 AM the building still draws about 477 kW, which is 87% of its weekday daytime load (549 kW between 10 AM and 4 PM), so it barely powers down overnight. Monthly use ranged from about 10,600 kWh per day in March to about 14,100 kWh per day in August. At this rate Bartley uses an estimated 4.4 million kWh per year. Figures come from 15-minute readings from Villanova Facilities' meter for the building.
