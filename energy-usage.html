Bartley Hall, measured

Real 15-minute meter readings from Villanova Facilities, December 2025 to October 2026.

Measured

Bartley Hall's meter recorded an average load of about 507 kW, or roughly 12,155 kWh per day. Its peak demand was 884 kW on September 1, 2026 at 4:30 PM. Between 1 and 4 AM the building still draws about 477 kW, which is 87% of its weekday daytime load (549 kW between 10 AM and 4 PM), so it barely powers down overnight. Monthly use ranged from about 10,600 kWh per day in March to about 14,100 kWh per day in August, and Bartley uses an estimated 4.4 million kWh per year. Cutting its overnight use (10 PM to 6 AM) by 10% would save about 141,129 kWh and about $14,446 per year at $0.10236 per kWh.
